Location Data Notice

This system requests device location when you refresh location or mark onsite attendance.

Purpose

Location is used to check whether the reported position is within a registered workplace boundary.

When location is recorded

Attendance coordinates are stored only when you complete check-in or check-out. Validation and risk checks record distance, accuracy and review signals, not raw coordinates.

Access and retention

Administrators and authorized auditors can access attendance reports and audit records. The organization must define and communicate its retention period and employee-data procedures.

Your rights and support

Contact your organization's HR team for information about access, correction and retention.

Management must review and approve this notice before employee use.
